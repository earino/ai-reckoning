## Cancelled, reduced or distressed

- Core Scientific holders rejected CoreWeave's $9B offer (October 2025).
- Blue Owl left the Oracle Michigan project; Blackstone and PIMCO replaced it (April 2026).
- Oracle and OpenAI did not take Crusoe's Abilene expansion; Microsoft took it. Microsoft dropped a letter of intent with Nscale in West Virginia; Anthropic took the site.
- **Project Jupiter (Oracle/Blue Owl, New Mexico).** Oracle sent a force majeure notice (September 2026). The $18B loan was quoted at 89-91 cents; HSBC sold $300M in the mid-80s; ING is auctioning $500M of data-centre loans. No default is reported.
- **Repricing.** CoreWeave loan spreads went from SOFR+2.25% (March) to +5.50% (August). Meta's El Paso bond yielded about 7.5% against 6.6% for Hyperion. Oracle's November bond paid 7% to 8.65%.
- Secondary reports say CoreWeave notes and CDS traded at distressed levels; its debt was near $47B at Q3 2026.

## Who bears the risk

- **Off-balance-sheet vehicles.** Meta's Hyperion and El Paso debt is owed by vehicles that Blue Owl and BlackRock control 80%. Meta has four-year leases, but its 16-year residual value guarantee returns much of the risk to Meta. Bondholders hold the rest.
- **Developer vehicles.** Oracle's sites are financed by Vantage, Blue Owl/STACK and Related entities. Banks and private credit bear construction and delay risk. Oracle's lease is the only support, and Oracle depends on OpenAI.
- **Backstops.** Google guarantees about $5.9B of Fluidstack leases (plus Hut 8). Nvidia buys CoreWeave's unsold capacity ($6.3B). Broadcom backs $30B of notes for Anthropic compute. Suppliers underwrite customer credit.
- **Chip-lease vehicles.** xAI leases GPUs from a Valor fund funded by Apollo and Nvidia; residual value risk sits with the fund and its lenders.
- **GPU-backed loans.** CoreWeave and IREN loans sit in SPVs secured on chips and single contracts. One CoreWeave loan outlasts its contracts, so lenders carry re-contracting risk.
